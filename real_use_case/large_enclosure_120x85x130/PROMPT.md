# Prompt

Create an enclosure with the following requirements:

- Height: 130 mm
- Width: 85 mm
- Length: 120 mm
- Wall thickness: 1 mm
- Bottom thickness: 1 mm
- At 70 mm height, add a horizontal internal plate attached to all four enclosure sides
- Internal plate thickness: 1 mm
- The plate faces upward (TOP)
- Add 6 holes with 35 mm diameter
- Hole arrangement: 3 columns × 2 rows
- All six holes must be complete circles and must not overlap
- Create a lid as an outer cap
- The body must enter inside the lid
- Lid insertion depth: 60 mm
- Lid wall thickness: 1 mm
- Use practical clearance so the body can slide into the lid

The final result should be a valid FreeCAD model suitable for further inspection and 3D-print preparation.
