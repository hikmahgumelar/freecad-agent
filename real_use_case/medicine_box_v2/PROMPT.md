# Real Use Case: Medicine Box v2

Create a print-ready medicine box as three independent FreeCAD parts.

Requirements:

- Body: 120 × 85 × 130 mm, 1 mm wall, 1 mm bottom.
- Internal plate ledge: 3 mm wide × 1 mm thick at Z=70 mm.
- Plate: 117.6 × 82.6 × 1 mm, six Ø35 mm holes in a 3 × 2 pattern.
- Plate clearance: 0.2 mm per side relative to the body.
- Cover: 122.5 × 87.5 × 61 mm, 1 mm wall/top, 60 mm insertion depth.
- Cover clearance: 0.25 mm per side relative to the body.
- Cover print orientation: closed face down, opening up.
- Plate print orientation: flat.
- Body print orientation: upright.
- No support required by design.
- Output must be three separate FCStd documents: cover, plate, and body.
- Each document must contain only its own intended solid/part.

The real-case evidence should show the generated geometry in FreeCAD and the final print-oriented views.
